Local Broadcast

Local TV stations' free live streams, found on each station's website by the local-broadcast sidecar and shown in Live TV. Stations airing a local team's game come first, and the Local Sports channel always plays whichever station has the game.

Sidecar

Tune in within a second instead of waiting 15 to 20 seconds: the sidecar finds every enabled station's stream ahead of time and renews it before it expires, in the background, one station at a time, never filling every slot, so tuning in doesn't wait for it. That runs Chrome for a few minutes about every half hour (every 10 minutes at most for stations whose stream links expire sooner), so it uses more CPU and memory. A station with nothing to play right now is checked less and less often, and still takes the usual time to show its "Nothing is streaming" screen.

Local sports

Uses each team's schedule and TV listings from ESPN. A game matches a station by network (a CBS game is on the CBS affiliate), call sign, name, or the station's sports aliases. The station's web stream may show its own programming instead when the network or league doesn't allow the game online.
ESPN team: the abbreviation or id from the team's ESPN page URL (e.g. den in /nfl/team/_/name/den, 38 in /college-football/team/_/id/38).

Coverage area

Enabled stations farther than this from your location stay out of Live TV. 0 or empty: no limit. Stations without a location are always included.
Empty: the market's center. Distances are to each station's city.

Sign-in for this station

For a station that streams only to signed-in accounts. The sidecar keeps the email and password encrypted and signs in through the site's form; Jellyfin doesn't store them. It stops at a CAPTCHA or an emailed login code, so sites that use those can't be signed in to automatically.
No account yet? Enter the email and password you want, tick the box below once you've read the station's site and its terms, and use Create the account for me. That works when the site's sign-up form asks only for an email and password; if it has a CAPTCHA or asks for more, create the account on the site yourself and save the sign-in here. Any confirmation email is yours to open.

Market defaults

Station lists and local teams to start from. Tick one or more markets and load them: a station in more than one market appears once, and the main market (the first one ticked) sets the coverage center and the time zone for newscast times.

Add a market

Saves your current stations and teams as a market of your own, to load again later or export.
A market file in the same format as the bundled ones (sidecar/markets/*.json in the project repository), e.g. one exported from another server.
